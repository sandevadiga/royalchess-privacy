RoyalChess Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026
App: RoyalChess for Android (com.chessbox)

The short version

RoyalChess is an offline chess game. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information. Everything the app remembers stays on your phone, and you can erase it at any time.

The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no crash-reporting service and no user accounts on a server.

Who we are

RoyalChess (package name com.chessbox) is published by Santhosh Devadiga. Contact: santhoshrdevadiga@gmail.com.

What the app stores on your device

To work, RoyalChess saves the following in the app's private storage on your phone (Android AsyncStorage). It is not sent anywhere.

  • Your nickname (alias), the country you pick and your skill level, entered during first-time setup.
  • Your progress: rating (ELO), XP, daily streak, and your win, loss and draw record.
  • A few counters used to decide whether to ever offer a rating prompt (games finished, wins, and when a prompt was last offered). They stay on your device.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, music and music track, theme (system, light or dark) and board style.
  • A random session identifier created on your device when you start. It is not linked to your identity, is not an account credential, and is never sent to a server.
  • Which launch illustration was shown last, so the next one is different.

Games in progress are kept in the app's memory while you play. We do not use your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, location or advertising ID, and the app never asks for those permissions.

What we collect and share

Nothing. RoyalChess has no server and makes no network requests in this version. No data about you is collected by us, and no data is shared with or sold to anyone.

After a win, Google Play may show its own rating sheet (at most a few times in the life of the app). That sheet is provided and operated by Google Play: RoyalChess does not receive your rating, your Google account or any information from it, and Google handles it under Google's own privacy policy. You can also rate at any time from Settings > Rate RoyalChess, which opens the Google Play listing.

The app includes no advertising or analytics software and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The libraries it uses (for example the chess rules library, graphics, sound and haptics) run entirely on your device.

Permissions explained

  • Vibration (VIBRATE): used for haptic feedback on moves, captures and check. You can turn haptics off in Settings.
  • Internet (INTERNET): this permission is declared in the app, but this version does not use it and makes no network connections. It is there for possible future features such as online play. If we add any feature that sends data, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before release, and describe what is sent and why.

The Update button in Settings opens the RoyalChess page in the Google Play Store (or your browser). Anything you do there is handled by Google under Google's own privacy policy.

Google Play and your device

Google Play may collect its own information about installs, updates and app crashes under Google's privacy policy. That is separate from this app, and we do not receive personal information from it. Android backup is switched off for this app, so its data is not copied to Google Drive.

How to delete your data

  • Uninstall RoyalChess: this removes all of the app's data from your phone.
  • Or open Android Settings > Apps > RoyalChess > Storage > Clear data (the exact wording varies by phone).
  • The Log out button in Settings ends your local session and removes the saved profile (nickname, country, skill level) and the setup flag, so setup starts again. Your win/loss record and settings may remain until you clear the app data or uninstall.

Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

Children

RoyalChess is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Since the app collects no data at all, no information about children is collected either. If you are a parent and have a concern, contact santhoshrdevadiga@gmail.com.

Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal, unmodified Android phone. The app does not send data over the network, so there is nothing to intercept. No method of storage is perfectly secure, and anyone with access to an unlocked or rooted device may be able to see app data.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles data (for example when accounts or online play are added), we will update this page, change the effective date and update the Google Play Data safety section before the new version is released. The current version of the policy is always at this address.
